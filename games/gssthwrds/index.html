GSSTHWRDS

Guess the words from their consonants alone.

The rules

  • Enter one word or phrase per line. One line will be shown per page.
  • Spaces, punctuation and vowels (A E I O U) are removed, only consonants remain: Banana split → BNNSPLT
  • Each line needs at least one vowel and one consonant; digits don't count as either.
  • Up to 3 hints: vowel count, vowel spots, then half the vowels.
  • Score = words guessed right out of the total.

      How to play: Party

      1. Host sets up. One person types the words (one per line), adds a category if they like, and presses Play. Everyone else looks away!
      2. Show the screen. Everyone sees only the consonants, e.g. BNNSPLT, and shouts out guesses.
      3. Need help? Tap Hint. First you learn the number of vowels, then where they go BNNSPLT, then half of them are filled in.
      4. Reveal and mark. Tap Reveal to show the answer, then tap ✓ Got it or ✗ Missed. Only "Got it" adds to the score.
      5. Keep going. Next moves on; Back revisits earlier words, where you can still change a mark. After the last word you'll see the final score.

      How to play: Solo

      1. Get a word list. Ask a friend to type the words (one per line) and an optional category, or paste a list you haven't read. Then press Play.
      2. Guess. Look at the consonants, e.g. BNNSPLT, type your answer and press Enter. Capitals, spaces and punctuation don't matter.
      3. Wrong? The box shakes and you get the next hint for free. Once every hint is showing, one more wrong guess reveals the answer and counts as a miss.
      4. Hints go in order: the number of vowels, then where they go, then half of them filled in. You can also tap Hint yourself.
      5. Give up? Tap Reveal and confirm. It shows the answer and counts as a miss. A correct guess reveals it automatically and scores a point. Then tap Next.
